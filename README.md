# Ola-Cab-Data-Insights
This project provides insights into a simulated ride-sharing dataset inspired by Ola cabs in Bengaluru. It explores booking trends, revenue analysis, cancellation reasons, and customer and driver ratings. Key findings include ride volume patterns, vehicle performance, and factors impacting customer satisfaction and revenue.

#

# Power BI - KPI

### 1. **Overall**
- **Ride Volume Over Time**: A time-series chart showing the number of rides per day/week.
- **Booking Status Breakdown**: A pie or doughnut chart displaying the proportion of different booking statuses (success, cancelled by customer, cancelled by driver, etc.).

### 2. **Vehicle Type**
- **Top 5 Vehicle Types by Ride Distance**: A bar chart ranking vehicle types based on the total distance covered.

### 3. **Revenue**
- **Revenue by Payment Method**: A stacked bar chart displaying total revenue based on payment methods (Cash, UPI, Credit Card, etc.).
- **Top 5 Customers by Total Booking Value**: A leaderboard visual listing customers who have spent the most on bookings.
- **Ride Distance Distribution Per Day**: A histogram or scatter plot showing the distribution of ride distances for different Dates.

### 4. **Cancellation**
- **Cancelled Rides Reasons (Customer)**: A bar chart that highlights the common reasons for ride cancellations by customers.
- **Cancelled Rides Reasons (Drivers)**: A bar chart that highlights the common reasons for ride cancellations by drivers.

### 5. **Ratings**
- **Driver Ratings**: A box plot visualizing the spread of driver ratings for different vehicle types.
- **Customer Ratings**: A scatter plot comparing customer and driver ratings for each completed ride, analyzing correlations.

## Description of Visuals

1. **Ride Volume Over Time**:  
   A time-series chart that shows how the number of rides varies over time (by day/week).

2. **Booking Status Breakdown**:  
   A pie or doughnut chart that shows the proportion of various booking statuses such as success, cancelled by customer, cancelled by driver, etc.

3. **Top 5 Vehicle Types by Ride Distance**:  
   A bar chart that ranks vehicle types based on the total ride distance covered.

4. **Average Customer Ratings by Vehicle Type**:  
   A column chart that shows the average ratings given by customers for different vehicle types.

5. **Cancelled Rides Reasons (Customer)**:  
   A bar chart that displays the common reasons customers cancel their rides.

6. **Cancelled Rides Reasons (Drivers)**:  
   A bar chart that displays the common reasons drivers cancel rides.

7. **Revenue by Payment Method**:  
   A stacked bar chart showing how the total revenue is distributed across different payment methods (e.g., Cash, UPI, Credit Card).

8. **Top 5 Customers by Total Booking Value**:  
   A leaderboard visual that highlights customers who have spent the most on bookings.

9. **Ride Distance Distribution Per Day**:  
   A histogram or scatter plot that visualizes the distribution of ride distances per day.

10. **Driver Rating Distribution**:  
    A box plot showing the distribution (spread) of driver ratings for different vehicle types.

11. **Customer vs. Driver Ratings**:  
    A scatter plot that compares customer and driver ratings for each completed ride to analyze correlations.
